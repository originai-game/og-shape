# sunroof cassette

A panoramic car sunroof cassette: a flat rectangular steel frame (side rails, front and rear headers, two thin guide rails along the sides) with a centre crossmember. Two glass panels sit on top, front and rear, each on a dark carrier with four slide shoes and a small cable anchor. Each panel slides 40 mm along the length (the front one forward, the rear one back).

Overall size (bounding box): 1260 x 840 x 48 mm (x, y, z). Z is up.
