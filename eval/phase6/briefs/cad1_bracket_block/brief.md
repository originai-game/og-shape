# bracket block

An L-shaped bracket plate, 20 mm thick: an 80 x 60 mm L with legs 20 mm wide and rounded outer corners (radius 8). Its bottom edges are chamfered 2 mm. A rectangular pocket (30 x 20 mm, 16 mm deep) is sunk into the top of the long leg next to the corner, and an 8 mm hole goes through the end of the long leg.

Overall size (bounding box): 80 x 60 x 20 mm (x, y, z). Z is up.
