# toaster

A two-slot toaster: a rounded brushed-steel housing narrowing towards the top, two bread slots with wire supports inside, small rubber feet. On one end: a browning dial that turns, a cancel button that pushes in 3 mm, and a lever tab that slides down 59 mm.

Overall size (bounding box): 155.11 x 306.55 x 179 mm (x, y, z). Z is up.
