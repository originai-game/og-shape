# stepped bracket

A stepped bracket extruded 30 mm thick from a profile: an upper flange on the left, a vertical web, and a lower flange to the right that steps back. A 7 mm hole goes through the lower flange, and a 4.5 mm hole with a 90 degree countersink (8 mm) goes through the upper flange.

Overall size (bounding box): 76 x 53 x 30 mm (x, y, z). Z is up.

Reference image: `reference.png`, one view of the object as another tool rendered it.
