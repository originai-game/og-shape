# lever knob

A lever knob on a support block: a hexagonal block with a square recess on top and a shallow bore underneath; on it a stepped hexagonal knob that narrows in bands towards the top, with a square lug underneath. The knob both slides down 19 mm and spins about the vertical axis.

Overall size (bounding box): 50.54 x 57.76 x 98.94 mm (x, y, z). Z is up.
