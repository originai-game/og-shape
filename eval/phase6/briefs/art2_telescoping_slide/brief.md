# telescoping slide

A wall-backed three-stage extension slide: a vertical wall plate with two screw heads, standoffs and a C-channel outer sleeve running out from the wall; a middle section slides out of the outer sleeve (260 mm of travel), and an inner blade with an end block slides out of the middle (220 mm).

Overall size (bounding box): 1100 x 240 x 300 mm (x, y, z). Z is up.
