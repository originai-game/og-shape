# mounting block

A machined block: an 80 mm cube with its edges chamfered 2 mm. A cylindrical boss (30 mm across, 30 mm tall) stands on the top centre with a 6 mm hole down it. A small rib (20 x 4 x 12 mm) sticks out of one side at the top. Four 5 mm holes are drilled into the side faces, and a square pocket is cut up into the bottom (12 mm margin, 20 mm deep).

Overall size (bounding box): 99.5 x 80 x 109.5 mm (x, y, z). Z is up.
