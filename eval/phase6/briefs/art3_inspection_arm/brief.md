# inspection arm

A long-reach inspection arm: a floor plate with a column and a turntable collar, then four flat aluminium arm segments (700, 580, 480 and 380 mm long) in a chain, each joined to the previous by a hinge fork with an orange cap. All four hinges turn about the vertical axis (about +-77 degrees). The last segment carries a camera with a lens and four orange LEDs.

Overall size (bounding box): 2606 x 300 x 488 mm (x, y, z). Z is up.
