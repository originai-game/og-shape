# curved tubes

A curved tube assembly: S-shaped round tubes (each two 90 degree elbows of a bent pipe, 254 mm across) stacked vertically. The lower tube is fixed; the upper pair of tubes spins about the vertical axis on top of it.

Overall size (bounding box): 702.17 x 495.47 x 1658.35 mm (x, y, z). Z is up.
