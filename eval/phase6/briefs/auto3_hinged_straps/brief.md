# hinged straps

Two long perforated metal straps (914 mm long, 41 mm tall, under 6 mm thick), each a thin plate with a rolled curl along its top edge and seven small holes along its length. One lies flat and fixed; the second is pinned at one end and spins about that pin.

Overall size (bounding box): 914.4 x 5.72 x 76.2 mm (x, y, z). Z is up.
