# telescoping mast

A telescoping mast on a wall-side support frame: a base plate, a tall rail and two inner guides with cross pieces; a mid mast slides up out of the support (380 mm), a top mast slides up out of the mid mast (340 mm), and a round rotary stage with a plate and a pin turns on top (about +-160 degrees about the vertical).

Overall size (bounding box): 280 x 220 x 1546 mm (x, y, z). Z is up.
